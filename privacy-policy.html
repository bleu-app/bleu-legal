Bleu

Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

Bleu is a personal tool for tracking your credit cards, rewards points, and air miles. This page explains what information Bleu collects, how it's used, and the choices you have — written in plain terms, without legal padding.

1Information We Collect

When you create a Bleu account, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password, plus any display name you choose to add.

Everything else in Bleu is information you choose to enter yourself: card names and details, transactions, spending categories, cash and bank accounts, merchant category codes, and recurring transaction templates. Bleu does not connect to your real bank or card accounts and never collects actual card numbers, bank credentials, or live transaction feeds — every figure in the app is something you typed in.

2Family Sharing

If you create or join a Family inside Bleu using an invite code, any cards, categories, accounts, or transactions you mark as shared become visible to the other members of that Family. Family membership is entirely optional and can be left at any time from Settings.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To run the core features of the app: storing your data, calculating points and miles earned, and showing your spending summaries.
  • To process your one-time purchase and confirm your access to the app.
  • To respond when you reach out through the in-app Send Feedback feature.

We do not use your data for advertising, and Bleu does not include any third-party advertising or analytics tracking.

4Payments

Bleu's one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Bleu never receives or stores your card number or other payment details. Purchase validation is handled by our payment processing partner, which confirms your purchase on our behalf.

5Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored with a secure, hosted database and authentication provider. Data is encrypted in transit, and access to your data is restricted by strict security rules so that only you — and, for anything you share, your Family — can read or edit it.

6Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run Bleu, such as our hosting and database provider and our payment processing partners, and only as needed to operate the app.

7Your Rights & Data Retention

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: Settings → Personal → Delete Account. This cannot be undone.

For any other request about your data, contact us using the details below.

8Children's Privacy

Bleu is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of Bleu after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at bleuapp.support@gmail.com.
